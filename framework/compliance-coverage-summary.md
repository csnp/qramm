## Changes to this document

October 31, 2026: Withdrawn. This summary gave coverage percentages for NIST PQC, CMMC 2.0, FedRAMP and FISMA, with an overall figure of 95.5%. They cannot be reproduced: no method was published, the ratings in its table do not produce the percentages shown, and many of the control identifiers they rested on are withdrawn or do not exist in the current editions of those frameworks. Its forecast timeline is removed for the same reason. The framework mapping remains in compliance-mapping.md and is being corrected against each publisher's data.
