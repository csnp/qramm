## Changes to this document

October 31, 2026: Withdrawn. This summary gave coverage percentages for NIST PQC, CMMC 2.0, FedRAMP and FISMA, with an overall figure of 95.5%. They cannot be reproduced. The scoring formula in compliance-mapping.md (Compliance Coverage Calculation), applied to the ratings in this summary's table, does not give the ITR, CMMC 2.0 and overall figures shown: it gives 81.25%, 87.5% and 93.75% where the table showed 88%, 91% and 95.5%. The formula also needs the number of Direct, Strong, Partial and Supporting requirements and the total for each cell, and the table gave one rating per cell and no requirement counts. Many of the control identifiers they rested on are withdrawn or do not exist in the current editions of those frameworks. Its forecast timeline is removed for the same reason. The framework mapping remains in compliance-mapping.md and is being corrected against each publisher's data.
